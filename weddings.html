What We Offer

From your first enquiry to the final farewell, our dedicated wedding planners guide you through every decision — venue selection, catering menus, floral design, photography, entertainment, and guest management. We coordinate with our trusted vendor network so you never have to juggle multiple calls on the most important day of your life.

  • Full-service wedding planning & day-of coordination
  • Custom floral design and decor installation
  • Catering partnerships (vegetarian, vegan, international cuisines)
  • Live music, DJ, and AV production
  • Bridal suite arrangements & honeymoon liaison
  • Photography & videography packages
  • Guest transportation & hotel block bookings

Our Planning Timeline

Great weddings are built on great planning. Here is a typical timeline we follow to ensure nothing is left to chance:

  1. 12–18 months out: Venue shortlisting, budget framework, style mood board
  2. 9–12 months out: Vendor confirmations, catering tasting, guest list
  3. 6 months out: Invitations, decor finalisation, rehearsal planning
  4. 3 months out: RSVP follow-ups, seating plan, day-of schedule
  5. 1 month out: Final walk-through, vendor briefings, emergency kit prep
  6. Wedding day: Our coordinator is on-site from setup to send-off
